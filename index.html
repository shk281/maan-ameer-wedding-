MAAN AND AMEER 💖

💍 A Beautiful Beginning of Forever 💍

Dear Maan and Ameer! ❤️

Aap dono ko shaadi ki bohat bohat mubarak ho! 💐 Allah aap dono ki zindagi ko mohabbat, khushiyon, sukoon aur barkat se bhar de.
Aapka rishta hamesha pyaar aur bharose se mazboot rahe. Har subah naye muskurate lamhe laaye aur har raat sukoon aur mohabbat lekar aaye.

Allah aap dono ko hamesha ek doosre ka humsafar banaye, har buri nazar se bachaye aur aapki jodi ko salamat rakhe. Ameen! 🤲❤️

💕 A Little Love Poetry 💕

Tum dono ki jodi sada muskurati rahe,
Mohabbat ki khushboo har pal aati rahe. 🌹

Har khwab tumhara haqeeqat ban jaye,
Har din tumhari zindagi Eid ban jaye. ✨

Ek doosre ka saath kabhi chhootne na paaye,
Tumhare pyaar ka diya kabhi bujhne na paaye. ❤️

Rab likhe tumhari kahani sitaron se haseen,
Maan aur Ameer raho hamesha khush-o-khurram, Ameen! 💖
💌 13 OCTOBER 💌

Made with endless love ❤️
